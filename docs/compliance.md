# Australian food-safety record checks

Checked 28 September 2026. Scope: selected records for Australian food service, caterers and related retail businesses. The operator records whether each site is category one, category two or unknown. Confirm scope and local requirements with the regulator. These rules do not apply the NZ Food Control Plan and do not certify compliance.

| Rule | Check implemented | Authority and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AU-SCOPE | Unknown category requires review | [FSANZ food-safety standards](https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/business/food-safety-standards). Category and prescribed activities determine which tools apply. Exemptions and state rules require separate review. |
| AU-TEMP | Cold above 5C, hot below 60C, missing readings and cooling outside two hours to 21C plus four hours to 5C prompt review | [FSANZ cooling and reheating](https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/business/food-safety/cooling-and-reheating-food) and [temperature control](https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/business/food-safety/keeping-food-at-the-right-temperature). These are default controls for potentially hazardous food. An approved alternative needs evidence and a reviewed rule change. Cooling observed_at is the actual 60C checkpoint, cooled_21_at and cooled_5_at are actual observed checkpoints. The CLI does not infer readings. Cooking, processing and transport require review against the business's approved method. |
| AU-FSS | Active category-one and category-two sites need an appointed supervisor with certificate evidence within five years and recorded availability | [FSANZ food safety supervisor factsheet](https://foodstandards.gov.au/sites/default/files/2023-10/Std%20322A%20-%20FSS%20Final.pdf). This checks records only, not the qualification, recognised issuer, actual availability or state-specific requirements. |
| AU-TRAIN | Missing training date or evidence reference on active staff prompts review | [FSANZ food handler training](https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/business/food-safety/food-handler-training). Evidence must cover safe handling, contamination, cleaning and sanitising, and personal hygiene or the required skills and knowledge. A date alone does not prove competence. |
| AU-RETAIN | Category-one temperature records with retention below three calendar months from the last entry are flagged | [FSANZ evidence tool](https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/business/food-safety/evidence-tool). Category one only. Other ways of demonstrating controls to an authorised officer can replace records in applicable circumstances. All record types are kept indefinitely by this base. The date is a review floor, never a deletion schedule. Back up files referenced by evidence_ref separately. |
| POLICY-CAL | Past calibration review dates | Operator policy reminder. The template does not invent a legal calibration interval. |

Opening checks, cleaning due dates and supplier approval dates are operator schedules. A missing daily opening record is not presented as a statutory breach. Temperature findings remain visible after an action closes, preserving the observation. Food storage, receipt, allergens, recalls, cleaning adequacy, vulnerable-person food service and local licensing have requirements beyond these checks. An inspector's acceptance of evidence and a food safety supervisor's decisions remain outside this base.

No automatic food-release, discard instruction or promise of an inspection pass is produced. Review the food, time history, actual method and source documents before making decisions.
